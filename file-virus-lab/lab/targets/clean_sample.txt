Topic: Principles of Secure Computing

Computer security fundamentally relies on the CIA triad:
1. Confidentiality: Ensuring that information is accessible only to
   authorized individuals and systems.
2. Integrity: Guarding the accuracy, completeness, and authenticity
   of data by safeguarding against unauthorized modification or deletion.
3. Availability: Ensuring authorized users have reliable and timely access
   to critical data and system resources.
